It is not open source, and it is not source-available. No licence to use, copy,
modify or distribute it is granted to anyone — see [LICENSE](LICENSE).

Access to the source, including access to a private repository, is for reference
only and grants no rights.

## Nothing is for sale

There is no commercial licence on offer, no price list, and no purchase path.
Any future licensing model is undecided and will be published here when it
exists, not before.

## Your data is yours regardless

None of this touches your archive. Your conversations, notes and files are
yours, stored as ordinary readable files on your own computer and exportable at
any time. Licensing governs the software, never your data.

## No payment code, ever

Mind Archive itself will never contain payment code, phone home, or ask you for
money while you are using it. That promise predates this licence and survives
it: the application holds no payment provider, no amounts and no checkout, and a
test fails if any provider's name appears in the interface. See decision D-038.

## Enquiries

Licensing questions, and anything this page does not cover, go to the project
contact address in [`docs/_data/support.yml`](docs/_data/support.yml).
